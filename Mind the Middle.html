Mind the Middle

Ten words flash by, one at a time. Type back what you remember, in any order.

The challenge

Memory research on the serial position effect finds that people recall the first and last words of a list far more often than the ones in between. Words 4 to 7 are worth double points here. Can you beat the dip?

  • 5 rounds, each faster than the last (2.0 s down to 0.9 s per word).
  • Order doesn't matter. Small typos still count.
  • 14 points per round, 70 in total.
